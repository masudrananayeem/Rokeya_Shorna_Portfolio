About

Engineer by training, researcher by curiosity.

I studied Computer Science & Engineering through both a Bachelor's and a Master's, and my work has moved between shipping production software and studying how machine learning models perceive patterns — particularly in images.

Profile

A little more about my path

I began my career supporting systems as a Technical Support Engineer, then moved into web development — designing, coding and maintaining production websites for an international project implementation team. Since 2024, I've been working as a Junior Programmer on GIS-based systems for a rural road master plan, where I handle system design, database queries, backups and data security.

Alongside that, my M.Sc. thesis applied machine learning algorithms to facial expression detection, and I co-authored a published paper on deep learning-based sentiment analysis for Bangla text. I'm drawn to work that sits at the intersection of dependable engineering and applied research.
